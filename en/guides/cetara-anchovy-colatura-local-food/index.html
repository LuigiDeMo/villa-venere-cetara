Living Cetara

Authentic Cetara: anchovy colatura, the sea and local food

In Cetara, the sea is not only a view. It is work, preservation and cooking. Colatura di Alici DOP expresses this identity more clearly than any slogan and invites travellers to understand the village through flavour, people and season.

By Villa Venere, with local guidance from Martina · 6 min read · Last checked August 6, 2026

The short answer

A food-focused day in Cetara can begin at the harbour, continue through the centre and local shops, and end with lunch built around the catch and colatura. Never assume that tastings, workshops or visits are available: contact producers and restaurants to confirm openings and arrangements.

  • Colatura di Alici di Cetara has protected DOP status and a defined production method
  • Taste it in a simple dish before treating it as a generic seasoning
  • Ask what fish is available that day
  • Producer visits and tastings need confirmation

Colatura: time, salt and the memory of the sea

Colatura comes from anchovies matured under salt and knowledge held by Cetara's families and producers. DOP status protects origin and method; start with the consortium's information and the label to understand what you are tasting.

Only a small amount is used in cooking. Tasting it with pasta, vegetables or bread reveals its savoury depth without masking the other ingredients.

Food shops, preserves and producers

Businesses in and around the village work with anchovies, tuna and preserves. A guide can tell this as a living craft tradition without turning every mention into an endorsement.

Opening times vary and not every shop offers visits. Confirm directly before planning a workshop or tasting.

Choosing where to eat

Rather than following one ranking, choose the experience: an informal lunch, traditional cooking, a seafood menu or a more elaborate dinner. Asking about the day's catch and dishes using colatura often starts the most useful conversation.

Reserve at weekends and during high season. Communicate allergies and dietary needs directly to the restaurant.

A slow day in Cetara

A morning at the harbour, a walk through the centre, a food-shop stop and an unhurried lunch fill half a day, leaving the afternoon for the sea or terrace.

It is one of the most important days of the stay because it proves that remaining in Cetara is not missing an excursion. It is living in the place you chose as home.

Frequently asked questions

What is Colatura di Alici di Cetara?

It is a condiment obtained by maturing anchovies under salt, protected by DOP status and closely tied to Cetara's production tradition.

Can I visit local producers?

Some may offer shops, tastings or experiences, but availability and arrangements must be confirmed directly.

Should I reserve restaurants?

It is advisable at weekends, during high season and for larger groups.
