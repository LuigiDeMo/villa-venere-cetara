Cetara to Ravello: bus, ferry and private driver

Ravello is not on the water: reaching its gardens and viewpoints means climbing above the coast. From Cetara, travel by bus, combine a ferry with a bus through Amalfi, or arrange a private driver.

The short answer

When services are operating, the most scenic public option combines a ferry from Cetara to Amalfi with a bus to Ravello. The all-road alternative follows the relevant SITA connections. A private driver is more comfortable for groups, limited mobility, early visits or evening returns.

  • Ravello always requires a road section
  • Ferry to Amalfi plus bus is the most scenic public option
  • The villas include steps and outdoor paths
  • Concerts and evening returns need dedicated planning

Three ways to reach Ravello

By public transport, follow the connections shown in current SITA schedules or reach Amalfi by sea and continue by bus. The latter separates the coastal journey from the climb and is enjoyable when ferries are operating normally.

A private transfer removes the connection and allows an agreed departure and waiting time. Confirm whether the quote covers a one-way ride, the return or hours at disposal.

Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone

Villa Rufolo and the gardens at Villa Cimbrone are distinct experiences and both deserve time. Combining them with the town centre and lunch is possible in one day, but requires comfortable shoes and an itinerary that is not too tight.

Check opening hours, tickets and closures on the official websites. Routes include uneven surfaces, climbs and steps; visitors with accessibility needs should contact the properties directly.

When to leave and how to build the day

A morning departure helps you visit the gardens before the hottest hours. Spring and autumn are particularly pleasant for walking; summer requires water, sun protection and more allowance for traffic and queues.

If your day includes a concert, never improvise the return. Check the last practical service or agree on a driver in advance.

An unhurried itinerary

Villa Rufolo in the morning, a walk through the centre, lunch and Villa Cimbrone later form a complete day. For a slower rhythm, choose one villa and leave space for a coffee or panoramic aperitivo.

Returning to Cetara closes the contrast between Ravello's elevation and the harbour: two landscapes of the same coast without changing accommodation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reach Ravello by ferry?

No. Ravello is in the hills. Travel by ferry to Amalfi and continue by bus or transfer.

Can I visit Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone in one day?

Yes, allowing a full day and enough walking time between the two properties.

Will a private driver wait during the visit?

It depends on the agreed service. Check whether waiting time, parking and the return are included.
