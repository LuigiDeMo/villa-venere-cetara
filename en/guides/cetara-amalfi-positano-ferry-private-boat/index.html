By sea

Cetara to Amalfi and Positano: ferry or private boat?

From Cetara's harbour, the coast changes perspective. A ferry makes the journey part of the day; a private boat offers more freedom, swimming stops and an itinerary built around your group.

The short answer

For a straightforward day in Amalfi, the seasonal ferry is usually the clearest option when the sea is settled. Positano may require a connection or a specific sailing, which must be checked for the actual date. A private boat suits groups, celebrations and days when the cruise itself is the main experience.

  • Check sailings and sea conditions the day before and again in the morning
  • Do not plan your return around the last possible connection
  • For a private boat, confirm port, fuel, duration and included services
  • Choose one principal visit ashore to preserve an enjoyable pace

The ferry: simple, scenic and seasonal

A ferry avoids coastal traffic and reveals towers, coves and villages as a continuous landscape. It works especially well for Amalfi and, when schedules and connections allow, Positano.

Timetables change during the season and sailings can be altered because of the sea. Book ahead during busy periods, arrive at the harbour with time to spare and know the bus alternative.

A private boat: freedom and time on the water

The value of a private charter is not collecting more towns. It is choosing the rhythm: an agreed departure, swimming stops, one visit ashore and the return towards Cetara. For couples and small groups, it can become the defining day of the stay.

Before confirming, ask in writing about departure and arrival port, boat model and capacity, skipper, fuel, duration, drinks, towels, cancellation policy and the alternative if weather conditions are unsuitable.

A balanced day

By ferry, a day can include Amalfi in the morning and Positano later only when the actual connections leave enough time. During high season, choosing one and experiencing it properly is often more satisfying.

By private boat, alternate sailing, one or two swimming stops and a single landing. Lunch can be ashore, on board when included, or back in Cetara.

The plan B when the sea changes

The sea genuinely decides the day. If sailings are suspended, Amalfi remains reachable by SITA bus, while a group may prefer a transfer. Every private charter should have clear cancellation or rescheduling terms.

Avoiding inflexible reservations immediately after your expected arrival reduces stress when transport is delayed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel from Cetara to Positano without a car?

Yes, in season using the sea connections available on your chosen date, sometimes with a change. Check the operator's current timetable.

Is a ferry or private boat better?

A ferry is best viewed as transport; a private boat is a personalised experience, especially for groups or special occasions.

What happens in bad weather?

Sailings and private cruises may change or be cancelled. Check service notices and understand the operator's terms before booking.
