Pompeii from Cetara: transport, visit and return

Pompeii asks for a different kind of Amalfi Coast day: an early start, comfortable shoes and enough time inside the site. Returning to the sea at Cetara makes the contrast part of the journey.

By Villa Venere, with local guidance from Martina · 8 min read · Last checked 6 August 2026

The short answer

Without a car, the clearest route generally goes through Salerno and continues by train towards Pompeii, using the connections available on the actual date. A private driver reduces changes and can be practical for families and groups. Book admission through the official channel and allow at least several hours inside the archaeological site.

  • Leave early and treat Pompeii as a full-day excursion
  • Buy tickets through channels indicated by the Park
  • Do not rely on very tight connections
  • An archaeological guide helps make sense of an immense site

Getting there without a car

From Cetara, reach Salerno by bus or, when available, by sea. Continue by train towards Pompeii, checking which station and final connection best suit your chosen entrance.

Because schedules change, this guide does not prescribe a fixed connection. Build the route for the actual day and leave time between arrival, train and entry slot.

When to choose a private driver

A transfer is useful for a larger group, children, particular mobility needs or a departure before convenient public transport. It may cover a one-way ride or include waiting for the return.

The quote should state vehicle, capacity, meeting point, tolls, parking, waiting and cancellation policy. An archaeological guide is normally separate from transport.

How long to spend inside Pompeii

Pompeii is a city, not a single monument. Several hours allow a coherent first route; a longer day offers more depth but can be tiring in hot weather.

A licensed guide helps select houses, public spaces and themes without spending your visit navigating. Otherwise, download the official map before arrival.

Heat, accessibility and the journey home

In summer, enter in the morning, carry water and sun protection, and plan pauses. The terrain is uneven and accessibility varies, so consult the Park's dedicated information.

For the return, check the last practical connection to Cetara without depending on one single combination. Via Salerno should remain a clear alternative.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Pompeii as a day trip from Cetara?

Yes. It requires a morning departure and a well-planned day using public transport or a private transfer.

How long do I need at Pompeii?

Allow at least several hours inside for a first visit, in addition to transfers and breaks.

Does the admission ticket include a guide?

Admission, guided visit and transport are normally separate. Check carefully what each offer includes.
