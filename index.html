Villa Venere is a beautiful villa overlooking the sea, located near the various beaches of Cetara and the town center. It has 3 bedrooms with Smart TV, 2 bathrooms, one with washing machine. A comfortable sitting room where you can eat meals with a beautiful sea view. A well-equipped induction cooker. It boasts a wonderful terrace overlooking the sea, from which you can admire the Tower and the Port of Cetara. It is also equipped with a private access to the sea.
